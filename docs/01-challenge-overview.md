## Context (from NASA public material)
- Farmers increasingly use NASA Earth science data to make planting, irrigation and harvest decisions.
- Water is central: soil moisture (SMAP), precipitation (GPM), groundwater (GRACE-FO), crop health (MODIS, ECOSTRESS).
- NASA Harvest (food security & agriculture program) works with USDA FAS and GEOGLAM on crop monitoring.
